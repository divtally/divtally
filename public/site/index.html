The DivTally browser extension prices your rares automatically — it runs the same trade searches right from your browser, so nothing here ever has to talk to the trade site. The site is fully usable without it; this just saves you the copy-paste. How it works →

Store listings are in review — meanwhile the identical build is on GitHub →

What it does
  • Runs each rare's trade search from your own browser, logged-out
  • Fills in the cheapest buyout and folds it into your total
  • Shares that price to the community cache (short-lived) so the next visitor sees it too
  • Open source — every line is readable in the repo and the store listing
What it never does
  • No login, no session cookie, no POESESSID — zero account risk
  • Touches only pathofexile.com/api/trade and this site — nothing else
  • No tabs, no browsing history, no all-sites access
  • No affiliation with or endorsement by Grinding Gear Games — a community tool
